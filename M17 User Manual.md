7.4 Photo capture and analysis
7.4.1 Photo capture and precautions
One-Click Shooting: Press the button once to take 3 consecutive shots, and the images will
be immediately processed. The operation is simple and quick. (After taking the photos, the
system will automatically detect the facial area based on the subject's face and avoid the
facial features' positions.)
Photography Guidelines:
• Hair Issues: The face area must be clean and free from foreign objects and hair when
taking the photo. Hair contains a large amount of pigment and may be misinterpreted as
pigmented spots, affecting data accuracy.
• Makeup Issues: Photos should be taken with no makeup. Cosmetics may cover surface
skin problems, leading to biased data and even obstructing the instrument's light, hiding
deeper skin issues and affecting data accuracy.
• Angle Issues: The person being photographed should rest their chin on the chin rest and
their forehead on the forehead rest, facing forward. The head should be adjusted properly to
prevent issues like looking down or up, which could cause inconsistent facial regions and
prevent data comparison, affecting data accuracy.
• Light Blocking Issues: When taking the photo, be sure to use a Hood to avoid external
light sources affecting the shooting environment and causing inaccurate data analysis.
• Hood Guidelines: It must completely block the light to prevent interference from external
light sources.
