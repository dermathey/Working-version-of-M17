massaging for 2-3 more minutes. To achieve more
exfoliation, peel combination may be left on skin
for up to 6 minutes. Turn off steam. Remove with
luke-warm steamed towel.
Note: If client has sensitive skin, reduce the manual exfoliation by
pre-mixing Peel 1 and 2. Let sit for up to 5 minutes.
